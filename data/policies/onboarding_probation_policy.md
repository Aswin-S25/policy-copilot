# Onboarding and Probation Policy

**Document Owner:** People Operations
**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** 1 January 2026

This policy describes how new employees are onboarded and how the probation period works.

# 1. Before the First Day

## 1.1 Pre-Boarding

New hires receive a welcome email **5 working days** before their start date containing their first-day schedule and equipment collection details. Background verification must be completed before the start date.

# 2. First Weeks

## 2.1 First Day

On the first day, new employees collect their laptop and access badge, meet their manager and are introduced to their onboarding buddy.

## 2.2 Onboarding Buddy

Every new employee is assigned an onboarding buddy from their team for the first **60 days**. The buddy answers day-to-day questions and is not involved in performance assessment.

## 2.3 Mandatory Training

The following training modules must be completed within **30 days** of joining:

- Information security awareness
- Data protection and privacy
- Code of conduct and anti-harassment

Access to production systems is granted only after all mandatory training is complete.

# 3. Probation

## 3.1 Probation Length

The probation period is **6 months** for all new employees. During probation, the notice period on either side is shorter than for confirmed employees, as stated in the employment contract.

## 3.2 Probation Reviews

Managers hold check-ins at the end of month **1**, month **3** and month **5**. A formal confirmation decision is made before the end of month 6.

## 3.3 Extending Probation

Probation may be extended once, by up to **3 months**, if the manager and People Operations agree that more time is needed to assess performance. The employee must be told the reasons in writing.

## 3.4 Leave During Probation

Employees on probation accrue annual leave normally but may take at most **5 days** of annual leave before their probation is confirmed, unless their manager approves more.
