# Data Classification Standard

**Document Owner:** Data Protection Officer
**Policy Code:** DCS
**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** 1 March 2026

All company information must be assigned one of four classification levels. The classification determines how the information may be stored, shared and disposed of.

# 1. Classification Levels

## DCS-1 Public

Information approved for public release, such as marketing material and published blog posts. Public information may be shared freely.

## DCS-2 Internal

Information intended for employees only, such as internal announcements, org charts and most wiki pages. Internal information may be shared with contractors who have signed a confidentiality agreement.

## DCS-3 Confidential

Sensitive business information such as customer contracts, financial forecasts, source code and unreleased product plans. Confidential information may be shared externally only with a signed non-disclosure agreement (NDA) and the approval of the information owner.

## DCS-4 Restricted

The most sensitive information, including customer personal data, credentials, encryption keys and payroll data. Restricted information must be encrypted at rest and in transit, and access is limited to named individuals on an approved access list.

# 2. Handling Rules

## 2.1 Labelling

Documents classified Confidential or Restricted must carry the classification label in the header or file name. Unlabelled documents are treated as Internal by default.

## 2.2 Email and Chat

Restricted information must never be sent by email or chat. It must be shared through the approved secure file transfer system. Confidential information may be emailed only to approved recipients.

## 2.3 Disposal

Restricted and Confidential paper documents must be shredded using the cross-cut shredders or the secure disposal bins. Digital Restricted data must be deleted using IT-approved secure deletion tools.

# 3. Responsibilities

## 3.1 Information Owners

Every dataset has an information owner, usually the head of the team that created it. The owner assigns the classification and reviews it at least once a year.
