# Wellbeing and Benefits Policy

**Document Owner:** People Operations
**Version:** 1.1
**Effective Date:** 1 January 2026

This policy summarises the health and wellbeing benefits available to employees.

# 1. Health Insurance

## 1.1 Enrolment

Employees are covered by the company group health insurance from their first day. Spouses and children can be added within **30 days** of joining or within 30 days of a qualifying life event such as marriage or the birth of a child.

## 1.2 Annual Health Check

Employees aged 30 and above are entitled to one free preventive health check-up per year at a partner clinic.

# 2. Mental Health Support

## 2.1 Employee Assistance Programme

The Employee Assistance Programme (EAP) provides up to **6 free confidential counselling sessions** per employee per year. Sessions can be booked directly with the provider; the company does not learn who uses the service.

## 2.2 Mental Health Days

In addition to sick leave, employees may take **2 mental health days** per calendar year without providing a reason or documentation.

# 3. Wellness Allowance

## 3.1 Quarterly Wellness Allowance

Employees receive a wellness allowance of **75 USD per quarter** that can be used for gym memberships, fitness classes, sports equipment or meditation apps. Unused allowance does not roll over to the next quarter.

## 3.2 Claiming the Allowance

Wellness allowance claims are submitted through the expense system with a receipt, in the same way as other expense claims.

# 4. Ergonomics

## 4.1 Ergonomic Assessment

Employees can request a free ergonomic assessment of their office or home workstation through People Operations. Recommended equipment is provided by the company.
