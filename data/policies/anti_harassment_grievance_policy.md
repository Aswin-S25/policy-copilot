# Anti-Harassment and Grievance Policy

**Document Owner:** People Operations and Legal
**Version:** 2.0
**Effective Date:** 1 January 2026

The company is committed to a workplace free from harassment, discrimination and bullying. This policy explains how concerns can be raised and how they are handled.

# 1. Scope

## 1.1 Prohibited Behaviour

Harassment includes unwelcome conduct related to a protected characteristic, sexual harassment, bullying, intimidation and repeated offensive comments, whether in person, in chat tools or at company events.

# 2. Raising a Concern

## 2.1 Reporting Channels

Concerns may be raised with any of the following:

- The employee's manager
- People Operations
- The confidential Ethics Hotline, which accepts **anonymous** reports 24 hours a day

If the concern involves the employee's manager, it should be raised with People Operations or the Ethics Hotline instead.

## 2.2 Informal Resolution

Where appropriate and where the employee agrees, minor concerns may be resolved informally through a facilitated conversation. Employees are never required to attempt informal resolution first.

# 3. Investigation

## 3.1 Investigation Timeline

People Operations acknowledges a formal grievance within **2 working days** and aims to complete the investigation within **15 working days**. Complex cases may take longer, and the employee will be kept informed.

## 3.2 Interim Measures

During an investigation, the company may take interim measures such as changing reporting lines or seating arrangements. These measures are not disciplinary.

## 3.3 Outcome and Appeal

The employee who raised the grievance is informed of the outcome in writing. Either party may appeal within **10 working days** of receiving the outcome.

# 4. Non-Retaliation

Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith or takes part in an investigation is prohibited and is treated as serious misconduct.
