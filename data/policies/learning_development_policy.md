# Learning and Development Policy

**Document Owner:** People Operations
**Version:** 1.2
**Effective Date:** 1 April 2026

The company invests in employee growth through a learning budget, certifications and study leave.

# 1. Learning Budget

## 1.1 Annual Learning Budget

Each confirmed employee has an annual learning budget of **1,500 USD** per calendar year. The budget can be used for courses, conferences, books and online learning platforms. Unused budget does not carry forward.

## 1.2 Approval

Spending under **300 USD** can be approved by the employee's manager. Spending above 300 USD also requires approval from the L&D team. Approval must be obtained before payment.

# 2. Certifications

## 2.1 Certification Reimbursement

Exam fees for professional certifications relevant to the employee's role are reimbursed from the learning budget. The company reimburses the **first two attempts** at the same exam.

## 2.2 Clawback

If an employee leaves the company voluntarily within **12 months** of a reimbursement above 1,000 USD, they must repay the amount on a pro-rata basis.

# 3. Study Leave

## 3.1 Study Leave Entitlement

Employees may take up to **5 days of paid study leave** per calendar year to prepare for or sit approved exams. Study leave is separate from annual leave and must be requested at least **2 weeks** in advance.

# 4. Internal Learning

## 4.1 Mentoring Programme

Employees can join the mentoring programme twice a year, in January and July. Mentoring pairs meet at least once a month for six months.

## 4.2 Conference Speaking

Employees invited to speak at an external conference must have their slides reviewed by their manager and, where company information is included, by the Communications team.
