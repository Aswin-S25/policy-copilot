# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

**Document Owner:** People Operations
**Policy Code:** RHW
**Version:** 1.3
**Effective Date:** 1 February 2026

This policy explains who may work remotely, how hybrid schedules operate and what is expected of employees working away from the office.

# 1. Eligibility

## 1.1 Who Can Work Remotely

Employees become eligible for hybrid working after they have successfully completed their probation period. Roles that require physical presence, such as facilities and hardware lab staff, are not eligible.

## 1.2 Hybrid Schedule

The standard hybrid schedule is **3 days in the office and 2 days remote** each week. Teams agree their shared in-office days with their manager. Fully remote arrangements require approval from both the department head and People Operations.

# 2. Working Remotely

## 2.1 Core Collaboration Hours

Remote employees must be available online during core collaboration hours of **11:00 to 16:00 local office time**, regardless of their flexible schedule.

## 2.2 Home Workspace

Employees working from home must have a private, quiet workspace where confidential conversations cannot be overheard. Company devices must not be shared with family members or other household members.

## 2.3 Home Office Equipment

Eligible employees may claim a **one-time home office equipment allowance of 400 USD** for a desk, chair or monitor. The claim must be submitted within **90 days** of the hybrid arrangement being approved. Equipment purchased with the allowance belongs to the employee.

# 3. Working From Abroad

## 3.1 Temporary Work Abroad

Employees may work from another country for a maximum of **20 working days per calendar year**, subject to manager approval and a tax and immigration check by People Operations. Requests must be submitted at least **4 weeks** before travel.

## 3.2 Restricted Countries

Working from countries on the company's sanctions list is not permitted under any circumstances.

# 4. Ending a Hybrid Arrangement

The company may end a hybrid or remote arrangement with **2 weeks' notice** if business needs change or performance concerns arise. Employees may return to full office working at any time by informing their manager.
