# IT Asset Management Policy

**Document Owner:** IT Operations
**Policy Code:** ITA
**Version:** 1.4
**Effective Date:** 1 January 2026

This policy covers how company hardware is issued, tracked, replaced and returned.

# 1. Issuing Equipment

## 1.1 Standard Equipment

Every employee receives a company laptop, a headset and a laptop bag. Engineers may choose between a Windows laptop and a MacBook. Additional monitors for the office are available on request.

## 1.2 Asset Tags

Every company device carries an asset tag in the format **ITA-** followed by six digits, for example ITA-104233. Employees must not remove or cover asset tags. The asset tag number should be quoted in every IT support request.

# 2. Replacement

## 2.1 Refresh Cycle

Laptops are replaced every **3 years**. Employees receive an email from IT Operations when their laptop becomes eligible for refresh.

## 2.2 Damaged Equipment

Accidentally damaged equipment is repaired or replaced at no cost to the employee. Repeated damage caused by negligence may be escalated to the employee's manager.

## 2.3 Peripherals

Keyboards, mice and adapters under **50 USD** can be ordered through the IT self-service portal without manager approval.

# 3. Returning Equipment

## 3.1 Leaving the Company

Employees leaving the company must return all company equipment within **5 working days** of their last day. IT Operations provides a prepaid courier service for remote employees.

## 3.2 Data on Returned Devices

Returned devices are securely wiped by IT Operations before being reissued. Employees must not attempt to wipe or reinstall the operating system themselves.

# 4. Personal Devices

## 4.1 Bring Your Own Device

Personal phones may be used to access company email and chat only after enrolling in the company's mobile device management (MDM) app. Personal laptops must not be used to access company systems.
